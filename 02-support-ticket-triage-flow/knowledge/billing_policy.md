# Acme Analytics - billing policy

## Charges and invoices
Plans are billed on the 1st of each month. An invoice is emailed after every successful payment.
Invoices can be downloaded under Settings > Billing > Invoices.

## Duplicate charges
A duplicate charge for the same plan and period is refunded in full. Refunds are issued to the original
payment method within 5-10 business days. Support can approve duplicate-charge refunds up to 500 EUR.

## Refund policy
Annual plans can be refunded within 14 days of purchase. Monthly plans are not refunded for partial months,
except for duplicate charges and service outages longer than 24 hours.

## Escalation
Refunds above 500 EUR and chargeback disputes are handled by the finance team and need a human decision.
